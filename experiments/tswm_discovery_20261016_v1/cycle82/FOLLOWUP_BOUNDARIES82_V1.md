# Closest new references before a high-dimensional composition experiment

These primary references were located after the C82 numerical protocol and results were frozen. They change the novelty checks needed before promoting local reuse into a research direction; no C82 model or score changes.

1. [UNICON: A foundation model of numerical intelligence with cross-disciplinary generalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28432v3), Wu/Yu/Yang, Aug12 2026 revision. Its primary abstract describes a frozen graph-example-conditioned predictor spanning multiple scientific/social disciplines, including held-out disciplines. This is direct competition to a broad 'general graph numerical foundation model' claim. Full task information contracts, rollout/intervention capabilities, code and checkpoint readiness remain to be audited.
2. [Graph In-Context Operator Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12725v3), Wu/Yu/Sun/Yang, Apr15 2026 revision. Graph message passing and example-aware positional encoding support geometric and example-count generalization; reported controlled comparisons use the same training data/steps. The abstract centers on air-quality spatiotemporal prediction. Do not infer intervention-valid latent dynamics or lack of such capability from that abstract alone.
3. [Chain of Operators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12318v2), Yang/Wu/Guo/Yang, Aug24 2026 revision. CHOP adapts frozen ICONs via explicit mathematical operations and multiple model calls, across PDE and air-quality tasks. It directly occupies programmable, inference-time composition without weight updates. A new method cannot claim that broad principle as its novelty; its unknown-mechanism/data contract and actual computation/evidence would have to differ materially.

The [authors' publication page](https://scaling-group.github.io/publications/) links these works. It is a discovery aid, not evidence that their methods were reproduced here. None was downloaded or executed in C82. The next research decision must read their full mechanisms and verify real release contents before choosing a new baseline or claiming a gap.

The current local FIM results are positive capability evidence, not proof that a new compositional foundation model is needed. Strong ordinary local models already explain this carrier. Any larger-system experiment must test a distinct capability with matched data, graph information, adaptation cost and genuinely held-out conditions.
