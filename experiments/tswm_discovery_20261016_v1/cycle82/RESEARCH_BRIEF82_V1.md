# C82 research decision and candidate lineage

The previous turn was progress: C81 completed an actual six-system frozen-feature screen, independently checked72 outcome records and9312 coefficients, and published the negative decision. The numerical experiment and its raw outputs remain unchanged. The active broad research objective is still unachieved.

Three candidate concretizations were compared in exploratory mode:

1. **Cross-plan stochastic coupling.** Parent: probabilistic world models and the difference between a forecast distribution and a same-episode counterfactual. Operator: instrument. WorldKernel, Twin Rollouts, counterfactual cocycles and causal flow methods directly occupy the generic issue. A narrower question about few paired trajectories and marginal-preserving noise alignment is testable, but lacks a differentiated mechanism. Status: needs mechanism; not selected for a textbook nonidentifiability demonstration.
2. **Dynamics reuse after a nonlinear observation change.** Parent: C46/C78 and Meta-iLaD/CNC transfer. Operator: transfer. Earlier static anchors, known view identities and scalar calibration do not settle unseen nonlinear partial-sensor transfer. However, generic encoder/decoder adaptation and unsupervised alignment have substantial precedent, and arbitrary unknown sensors are not identifiable. Status: needs a precise observable contract and mechanism. Retained as a broader alternative, not assumed solved or novel.
3. **A joint dynamics prior reused as a local mechanism.** Parent: C80's qualified callable field model, C81's failure of a readout-only retrofit, and FIM-ODE's dimensionality/prior limitations. Operator: refine+combine. In a sparse larger system, a node's direct inputs can be observed although their own neighborhood dynamics are open. A low-dimensional joint autonomous prior and a target-only conditional mechanism prior then encode different assumptions. This is the cheapest new discriminating test available from the actual qualified substrate. Status: needs evidence and closest-work differentiation. Selected for C82, under a frozen clean-data protocol.

The selected route is revised by its strongest overlap challenge: classical controlled system identification already models outputs with observed exogenous inputs; CompNO/ICON and function encoders already support composition. Therefore the possible contribution is not 'add actions' or 'compose modules.' It would have to establish a practical failure and a scalable inference/data-generation remedy for reusing a frozen joint prior under open neighborhood conditioning. No such remedy is accepted at this stage.

The evidence challenge is equally important: full-state FIM receives extra information, and changing boundary dynamics changes trajectory geometry. A full-versus-local gap alone is insufficient. C82 uses identical central mechanisms, common query coverage diagnostics, clean data and strong target-only weak controls; any positive signature still needs matched-geometry/alternative-prior and composed-network followups.

Generic CCF-A/ML venue target is assumed; no acceptance probability or submission readiness is claimed. Single5090 is the available main compute resource; the first screen uses CPU and the already verified13M checkpoint to preserve the ongoing original-budget GPU reproduction. Data and raw arrays stay on the server15TB project storage; GitHub receives code, protocols, compact evidence and reports only.
