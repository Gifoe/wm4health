# C82 primary-source boundary audit

Checked on2026-10-18. These sources guide the candidate choice; this cycle does not reproduce every work listed. A paper's existence is not evidence that it solves every contract below. Conversely, a new application of an established mechanism is not automatically a new method.

## Selected candidate: closed joint prior versus open target mechanism

[Foundation Inference Models for Ordinary Differential Equations](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.08733v2) defines an autonomous ODE inference task on observed state coordinates. It pretrains on degree-three polynomial systems of dimension at most three; its discussion explicitly connects higher-dimensional data-generation difficulty to unstable polynomial trajectories. The pinned released checkpoint is the C80-qualified substrate. A local neighborhood extracted from a larger system is outside this contract when its own dynamics depend on omitted boundary states, even if one target component's parents are visible. The latter is the C82 hypothesis, not an author-reported failure.

[Compositional Neural Operators for Multi-Dimensional Fluid Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11691) and the [ICLR version](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=VFLwvtTwWF) already pretrain elementary operator blocks and adapt their composition. Governing-equation operators, physical parameter vectors, and physics residuals play an explicit role. This occupies generic composition and block reuse. It is different from inferring an unknown target field from local observations whose boundary dynamics need not be modeled. An OpenReview browser challenge prevented fresh full-PDF retrieval in this pass; primary abstract and indexed manuscript passages establish this limited distinction. Code execution not verified.

[Zero-Shot Transfer of Neural ODEs](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.08954), with [actual code](https://github.com/tyler-ingebrand/NeuralODEFunctionEncoder), already learns reusable vector-field/flow bases and estimates coefficients from context increments, including residual and controlled applications. [ICL-SysID](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13411) already studies in-context input-output identification, and classical controlled-system identification is an essential ordinary alternative. Thus 'model the input as exogenous' or 'learn a frozen conditional basis' is not an innovation claim. A surviving contribution must establish a distinct practical transfer failure and an independently validated scalable remedy, rather than rename these operations.

[From Observation to Insight: Mechanistic World Models and the Quest for Autonomous Discovery](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12474) is a conceptual blueprint centered on variables, reusable mechanisms and bindings. It motivates mechanism-oriented evaluation but is not an executed solution for the C82 contract. C82 similarly does not establish a mechanistic world model merely by exposing a vector-field API.

## Alternative: observation changes

[Meta-iLaD](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/ye26p.html), ICML2026, learns an environment variable from few-shot contexts and claims joint identifiability of state, environment and transition under its theorem assumptions. The primary abstract does not establish arbitrary unknown changing sensor maps; the full theorem's exact conditions remain to be checked before using that boundary as a gap. The [author repository](https://github.com/GoForIt-YYB/Meta-iLaD) was previously audited as paper/poster/README material, not a completed native reproduction.

[World Models for Cross-Machine CNC Transfer under Partial Sensor Overlap](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.16071), Sep2026, explicitly tests command-conditioned transfer from17 source sensors to a target sharing10. Its source-only selection and target-once protocol matter; a normalization diagnostic does not establish general nonlinear observation transport. C46's known views and shared static anchors likewise do not settle post-training unseen observation operators. The alternative remains a needs-mechanism question, not rejected simply because alignment methods exist.

## Alternatives with direct overlap: causal retention and cross-world coupling

[Causal Retention in Interactive Agents](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.30650), Sep2026, fixes mechanism probes for action, context, direct target, value and delay, and studies selective updates after shifts. Its continuous protocols use paired interventions; the TD-MPC2 result is a mechanism-response evaluation. The text distinguishes the retained queryable memory from task sufficiency. One cannot claim that source-domain probes or low forecast error establish editable mechanism retention; nor can its response-sign score be silently treated as full target rollout recovery.

[WorldKernel](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.10934), Jun2026, directly studies unidentified cross-world couplings despite identified predictive/interventional quantities and develops bounds/constraints. [Twin Rollouts](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.08982), Aug2026, formalizes shared-prefix/shared-noise counterfactual branches; the manuscript states that experiments are forthcoming. These substantially occupy the generic 'same forecast marginals need not imply the right counterfactual pair' observation.

[Counterfactual Generation via Flow Matching: Coupling-Sensitive End-to-End Rates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01193), Oct2026, already learns source-target couplings with doubly robust/sample-split training and coupling-sensitive sampler bounds; [official implementation](https://github.com/HWDance/DeconfoundingFlowMatching). Its target is counterfactual outcome distributions under identifying assumptions, not automatically the physical same-noise trajectory coupling of every temporal world model. [Counterfactual Cocycles](https://hwdance.github.io/files/Counterfactual_cocycles_.pdf) directly treats composition consistency of transport-based counterfactual maps. A new temporal coupling study needs a differentiated mechanism and estimand before execution; C82 does not run a textbook Gaussian gauge counterexample and call it new.

## Current inference boundary

The selected clean-data closure screen is an exploratory transfer test on four related mechanisms and two initial-condition draws. Even a strong signature would not prove that closed pretraining is the unique cause, or that existing input-output identification and compositional models fail. A full-state diagnostic receives extra context, while regime changes also alter context geometry. Common query support and target-only controls reduce those ambiguities but do not eliminate them. No final novel direction has been accepted.
