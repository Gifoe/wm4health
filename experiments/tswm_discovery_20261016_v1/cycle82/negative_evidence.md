# negative evidence

“局部不闭合就会使预训练场推断失效”没有得到当前实验支持。不能继续把这个解释当作既定研究缺口。
Generic closure failure is not inferred from an open neighborhood alone. Ordinary controls, actual local FIM successes and all paired results are retained. Four related polynomial mechanisms with clean observations do not settle general hidden-state or high-dimensional inference.
