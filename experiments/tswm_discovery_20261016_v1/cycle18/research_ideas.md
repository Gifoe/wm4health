# Current research triage

No accepted research idea. The target remains a general reusable time-series world-model framework, not performance tuning on Lorenz or any dataset.

The latest evidence strengthens the baseline side: a released10k-parameter autonomous model can produce useful long-run simulations with one fixed model. A proposal to merely replace forecast heads by recurrent latent dynamics would not distinguish itself from existing work.

A possible next search area is faithful reusable dynamics when the supplied evidence only partly identifies the system, for example incomplete sensing or short transients. This is a question to screen, not a novel contribution. Delay embeddings, nonlinear observers, latent-parameter inference, switching SSMs and generative NPs are direct strong controls; previous cycles already eliminated several easy variants. Do not revive those without a precise new distinction.

Before another fit, inspect existing local source and closest prior art, then freeze one decisive falsification test under a defensible information contract. If existing methods succeed, retain the negative evidence and change scope. No new architecture is justified by current results.
