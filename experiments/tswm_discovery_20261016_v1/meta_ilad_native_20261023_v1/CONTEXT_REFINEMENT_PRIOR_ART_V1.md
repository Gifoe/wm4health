# Native source and recent direct prior art

Retrieved2026-10-23. [T2-z](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.22364v1), published15Oct, learns environment latents and directly optimizes them at inference with frozen shared world-model parameters. Its reported backbone is Wan2.2-TI2V-5B and its objective is flow matching. This directly rules out presenting “optimize a compact environment latent at test time” as our innovation. No T2-z code, weights or benchmark have been executed in this project.

Our next diagnostic keeps the already executed Meta-iLaD source/checkpoint and uses standard observed-image latent fitting. It is an existing-principle transfer for failure attribution. Earlier Bayesian context aggregation and state-inference evidence remains in[the historical boundary](../cycle69/LITERATURE_DELTA69_V1.md). Even a positive diagnostic would not establish a new method or a general research gap.
