- Generic modular dynamics, hidden-entity inference, state-conditioned routing and graph posterior inference have direct prior art. None is an accepted new contribution here.
- Earlier C41-C46 controlled composition already had strong ordinary alignment/state-space repairs. A new name, nonlinear encoder or added expert does not establish a new required mechanism.
- The recent continual-composition benchmark explicitly identifies privileged full-task encoder exposure and uncontrolled capacity. Those claims must be attributed to its authors and reproduced before use as our evidence.
- DynaMix was actually executed in C18; repeating its demonstrations would not be a new baseline. The historical index corrects stale retrieval and distinguishes citation from execution.
- The current spring subset is row-disjoint but not structurally disjoint; it does not measure unseen mechanism recombination. A native block-rolloutVAL result is not a no-future-reset simulation result.
- One execution qualification is not training sufficiency. Its very large initialVAL error cannot support a new framework or a stable deficiency. Formal curves and independent mechanism evidence are still needed.
