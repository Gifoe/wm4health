# Joint observation and dynamics change: current evidence boundary

18 October 2026. This is a targeted primary-source and code screen, not a systematic survey or a new experimental result. It narrows candidates A/B; it does not accept a new contribution. No final direction is accepted. The ongoing C71 campaign and frozen assessment are unchanged.

## Closest sources and what they establish

**SplitJEPA**, [paper v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.12349v1), sections 2-3 and appendix C; [official source](https://github.com/splitjepa/splitjepa.github.io/tree/a8d65ecbc3dd173d3130f588e685861551a79294). Its identification result assumes stationary isotropic Gaussian predictive structure and correspondences that preserve one block while exposing full-rank variation in the other. Seven selected source files, 22,758 bytes, were actually read and checked against Git blob hashes; hashes and scope are in SOURCE_SCREEN_SPLITJEPA71_V1.json. The synthetic path freezes a learned inverse-coupling encoder, applies ZCA and obtains a rotation from pair-difference covariance. The robot training path uses a separate matched-pair loader alongside temporal prediction. This directly covers paired invariant/variant separation. It does not demonstrate unpaired sequential interface discovery. That missing setting is a differentiation question, not evidence the method fails there. No encoder training, robot experiment or Lean build was performed here; author source is not redistributed.

**JEPA-TTT**, [paper v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00722v1), sections 3 and 6. It retains predictor adaptation across episodes while holding the encoder and reward interface fixed. The studied dynamics shift is fixed during deployment; sequences of changes and return to old dynamics are excluded from its evaluation. Dense windows, more updates and replay are already tested controls. Therefore a generic test-time predictor update is not novel. Simultaneous observation/dynamics changes and retention require a different experiment, but their omission cannot by itself establish failure. We read the method and limitations; we did not run author code.

**Compositional continual-world-model benchmark**, [paper v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.22055v1), sections III-E and IV. Its encoder diagnostic explicitly uses demonstrations from all tasks before sequential training. Its progressive experts and replay/regularization controls already expose the representation-drift/reuse issue. A method avoiding future-task encoder access addresses a recognized question, so a contribution needs a new learning interaction and independent evidence, not merely a less privileged implementation. No benchmark execution is claimed here.

**Stratified retention**, [position paper v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03713v1), sections 5 and 7. It proposes separately tracking persistent-knowledge retention and adaptation latency, and explicitly reports a framing/protocol rather than experiments. This supports measuring appropriate revision separately from forgetting. It does not supply a trained algorithm that solves joint observation/dynamics shifts. Its category of immutable physics is not used as a universal truth here: observation changes, system parameters and actual mechanism changes must be defined by each experimental contract.

**R2-WAM**, [paper v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.18038v1), sections 3-4 and limitations. Its generative replay is rectified by completion truncation and observation/action consistency filtering. Its video/action forgetting diagnosis is actual author-reported evidence, not our execution and not a numeric time-series result. Ordinary replay is therefore a required strong control; the video benchmark does not establish that unpaired time-series interface revision is solved. We read the method, diagnostic and limitations; no source execution is claimed.

**Growing world models**, [author position-paper page](https://anxu-its.github.io/publication/2026-oak-continual-world-model). Structural state/option expansion is already a public research agenda, not a newly discovered axis. The page describes hypotheses and an agenda; it does not establish performance of a competent complete algorithm. Thus generic latent expansion cannot be claimed as a new idea, and its mere proposal cannot justify rejecting every concrete implementation of that problem.

**Meta-iLaD**, [ICML proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/ye26p.html) and [author repository](https://github.com/GoForIt-YYB/Meta-iLaD). The abstract covers few-shot environment inference and joint identification of states, environment variables and dynamics. Current inspected repository contents are README, paper and poster; README announces future code. Primary full-PDF retrieval through the browser failed due its content type and OpenReview challenge. This screen therefore does not infer unverified theorem assumptions or claim a native reproduction. Adding an environment variable alone overlaps its stated mechanism.

## Minimum viable question retained for falsification

Can a time-series world model preserve and reuse learned dynamics when observation interfaces and mechanisms arrive sequentially, without paired views or exposure to future-task observations? This is a provisional question, not an accepted gap. The sharper issue is deciding when an interface change can be absorbed by calibration and when the available observations require a richer predictive state. A generic stable/variant split or larger latent vector is insufficient method novelty.

The mechanism must be evaluated through observable future behavior. If two latent explanations are observationally indistinguishable under the supplied history and future input support, requiring the learner to select a unique physical explanation is invalid. Report predictive uncertainty or an equivalence class rather than manufacture an identification failure. Conversely, good representation alignment alone cannot establish correct future dynamics.

Three separate conditions are needed: observation-only changes with sufficient information; dynamics-only changes under a stable interface; and joint changes, with informative and information-losing sensor cases distinguished. Return-to-previous-condition trials separate preserved knowledge from appropriate adaptation. Calibration traces, future controls, masks and history length must be identical across compared methods. Known mappings or true hidden states are upper-bound diagnostics, not information granted only to a candidate.

The first controls are complete frozen-dynamics observers with learned observation adapters, unrestricted recurrent/state-space adaptation with replay, and a factorized switching state-space model with observation and dynamics contexts. For state-expansion claims, include a larger fixed-state model at matched final capacity and training budget. For alignment claims, include nonlinear alignment as well as linear rotation. These are ordinary baselines, not proposed innovations. Existing C41-C46 affine controls and C20-C21 response-projection results remain negative evidence; changing notation or repeating their carriers is not progress.

## Decision and next actual work

Keep one focused development route from A/B: sequential observation/dynamics reuse. Do not promote the broader words stable representation, state expansion, or component reuse as a contribution. Its unresolved mechanism and independent evidence remain explicit. The measurement-only alternative C stays secondary because graph-overlap hygiene is insufficient novelty.

C71's full-budget simulation assessment will establish whether the chosen reusable dynamics baseline competently simulates future trajectories; it cannot validate the joint-shift question by itself. Its TRAIN graph cohort covers all34 five-node isomorphism classes. No post-training split can change that exposure. The qualified four-row hidden-state observer is in-sample execution evidence only.

While the formal fits run, the next useful independent action is to identify a prospective joint-shift data contract and competent complete ordinary controls. Existing physical-system corpora must be checked for truly unused recordings and whether a claimed observation change is measured or artificially constructed. Historical C69 test records cannot be relabeled as a new untouched cohort. If no independent data or identifying information is available, change the experiment rather than assume latent truth can be recovered. Only an observed residual surviving those controls would justify method design. No such residual or new algorithm is established by this screen; overall goal ACTIVE/UNACHIEVED.
