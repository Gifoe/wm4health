# Research branches after the ordinary-model screen

These are exploratory leads, not three final recommendations. No new gap or method is accepted, and no reported effect is assumed before C46 EVAL verification. The purpose is to prevent another cycle of architecture packaging or dataset tuning.

## A. Rescue of observation/dynamics reuse: noninvertible evidence

Parent: C42/C46 composition question; operation: refine. Current C46 views are noisy but injective functions of the4D state. Static anchor alignment can therefore be unusually effective. A harder, potentially useful capability would reuse mechanisms across sensors that individually reveal different noninvertible parts of state, so temporal belief inference is necessary. This is a legitimate narrowing, **not yet novelty**. The strong control must include ordinary context-aware variational inference and sensor-specific likelihoods; a stateless encoder or first-k-coordinate trick would be inadequate.

Direct older boundary: [Multi-View Reinforcement Learning](https://discovery-pp.ucl.ac.uk/10110448/1/8422-multi-view-reinforcement-learning%20%281%29.pdf) already learns shared dynamics across transformed/noisy state descriptions and transfers control. The author's [Causal World Models thesis](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10155513/) explicitly describes multi-view world models. Do not present multi-view shared dynamics as a newly discovered problem. The missing distinction would have to concern an explicit learning interaction, information regime and independently useful behavior; none is established here.

## B. Different problem angle: world dynamics versus the observation policy

Parent: continuous-time observation/dynamics separation; operation: invert the usual exogenous-timestamp assumption. In deployed sensor/clinical systems, *when* a measurement arrives can depend on the hidden process and collection policy. A change in collection frequency need not be a change in the physical mechanism. A candidate capability is to keep physical dynamics reusable while adapting the observation process and the meaning of “no measurement.” This concerns a world-model factorization and inference contract, not one dataset's score.

Strong direct boundaries already found: [Lange et al., Biometrics2015](https://vnminin.github.io/papers/Lange2015.pdf) models a latent continuous-time Markov process with a state-dependent observation-time process and EM. [HyperNSDE,2026-10-05](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.07383v1) jointly models neural stochastic longitudinal dynamics and latent-state-dependent observation intensity. Thus “add a point process to a latent SDE” is already covered. HyperNSDE has only been screened here, not reproduced or fully audited.

Further direct boundary: [TESAR-CDE, ICML2023](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/vanderschueren23a.html), with [MIT official code](https://github.com/toonvds/TESAR-CDE), already formulates informative sampling as covariate shift and uses inverse-intensity weighting in a neural CDE for treatment-outcome forecasting. Its SAR/strong-SAR and treatment-observability assumptions must be read before extrapolating to a hidden-state-dependent clock. Thus weighting by observation intensity or adding a sampling head is not an original method either. No code from that repository has been executed in C46.

Possible discriminating question, still needs a complete source check: after a sampling-policy change, can a learned model decide which factor to adapt without incorrectly changing its physical dynamics? First compare ordinary joint-likelihood models with an appropriate policy/intensity adaptor and matched information. If those solve it, no extra mechanism is justified. If the two shifts are observationally indistinguishable, state the identification limit and required extra evidence; do not pretend an architecture can recover unavailable information. A new recipe would need a concrete finite-data/compute benefit beyond these complete controls and a fresh held-out protocol. No training for this lead has started.

## C. Different application/evidence angle: plan discrimination

Parent: WorldTS/TSWM action-conditioned simulation; operation: transfer, only after checking assumptions. Preserving action-dependent differences rather than factual forecasting accuracy is potentially relevant, but highly occupied. [AD-WM,2026-09-24](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.30264v1) already combines latent residual dynamics with inverse action recovery and a normalized action-recovery objective for counterfactual MPC. Reusing that loss under another name would not be a contribution. The original [TSWM repository](https://github.com/HaochenZhang717/TSWM-Official-Repo) is also a mandatory boundary. No action/control requirement is being imposed on the user's overall time-series world-model goal.

Development priority: complete and explain C46 first. Do not add C46 seeds or tune its EVAL. If the ordinary result remains sufficient, search one sharply differentiated capability using the current primary sources before launching another large neural sweep. A, B and C require different evidence; combining them into one oversized framework now would be unsupported.
