# C46 priority decision: simple neural factorization is not the new framework

All8 fresh fits/27250 updates and the full frozen evaluation completed. Independent verification covers192 raw groups/15360 origins. Held-out joint energy averages: O0D0=.559250, O0D1=.549072, O1D0=.556522, O1D1=.552041; ordinary aligned PCA/LGSSM=.440893. The error-scale interaction changes sign across initialization (−.015536,+.004144), as does the seen-cell interaction. There is no stable extra benefit supporting the proposed simple joint factorization story.

TRAIN/VAL-only auditor probes replay the original generator exactly and recover far more current-state information through the cheap model's affine probe (R2=.9102) than through these neural states (R2=.1863–.3044). This points to representation/inference/optimization issues worth acknowledging, not a newly demonstrated universal limitation. O1 simultaneously restricts recognition and generative observation context; it is not a pure decoder ablation. One selected checkpoint is at its final update; no fit hits hard8000. We do not assert all neural training possibilities have been exhausted.

Decision: **deprioritize simple observation/dynamics factorization as the main proposed contribution.** Do not add more seeds, retune this EVAL, rename the ordinary composition, or make the synthetic carrier harder merely to force failure. This is one injective-view carrier with static alignment information; it does not refute every possible reusable world-model recipe. Known-component recipes remain eligible if a necessary interaction and independent utility are actually shown.
