# C46 targeted prior-art boundary, 2026-10-17

This is a targeted supplement to the earlier literature packets, not a complete six-month survey. The experiment is an ordinary neural-composition falsification screen. It establishes no new framework yet. Publication scope remains a reusable time-series world model; one controlled carrier is insufficient.

Additional RP-GSSM code boundary: `dists.py:LGStationaryParam` uses “stationary” to mean time-constant parameters, has independently configurable initial/invariant constraints, and `ACLGParam` explicitly sets `stay_at_invariant=False` with action-dependent transitions. Thus neither a class name nor the paper's whitened-prior lemma supports a claim that the implementation lacks nonstationary/action-conditioned priors. `rpm.py:ConstrainedIVFreeEnergy` uses the current prior chain in its normalizer terms. No evidence here establishes an arbitrary-prior reuse failure.

Rejected source handling: secondary paper summaries, broad-search MDPI results, unrelated acronym matches, and a wrongly guessed PMLR URL are not evidence. In particular `v162/zhao22d` is a PDE inverse-problem paper, not the compositional world-model citation initially sought. RP-GSSM v2 was not available; only v1 is used.

Research inference: the potentially useful capability is probabilistic reuse of observation evidence and dynamics under missing temporal combinations. The present recipe is an ordinary control. A contribution would require either a distinct necessary learning interaction with useful independent generalization, or a clearly differentiated problem/evidence contribution. Generic SSM expressivity does not prohibit such work, but neither a module diagram nor wins over an intentionally unstructured control prove novelty. Episodic recomposition remains an unselected, overlap-uncertain lead; MAML/modular NRI and ordinary multi-view training are mandatory comparisons before that recipe could be promoted.
