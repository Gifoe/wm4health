# C88 closest-work boundary (2026-10-19, before paired-plan results)

- [MTS3, NeurIPS2023](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.18534v1): the slow task prior for window k is conditioned on the aggregated actions of that window; that task informs fast predictions throughout the window. This is the paper's temporal abstraction, not merely an accidental file-offset typo. The [pinned implementation](https://github.com/ALRhub/MTS3/blob/ab971c0ec43df7664c4b727ca69d5b8dd0cab5da/agent/worldModels/MTS3.py) implements that dependence. A primitive-intervention nonanticipation test is a stricter transferred simulation contract, not a claim that the authors' observational action-conditional results are fabricated. The experiment must measure trained behavior and ordinary causal alternatives.
- [Time Series World Models: Accuracy and Mechanism Consistency](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01842v1) already distinguishes prediction accuracy from declared action-response consistency. Our C79 previously found no plan-tail anticipation in its tested native models. A general forecasting-versus-simulation narrative or sign penalty is therefore not novel here. C88 tests a different hierarchical model; one counterexample cannot establish a field-wide defect.
- [The Intervention Gap in Latent World Models](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.29998v1) uses environment-executed action contrasts and capture/propagation qualification, including support-dependent failures. C88 shares the need for real simulator branches and competent representations. The retrieved sections do not establish the exact macro-window prefix test; that absence is not proof of novelty. A generic intervention audit already has substantial prior art.
- [Hierarchical Planning with Latent World Models](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.03208v2) compresses primitive chunks into macro-actions and predicts endpoint waypoints used as lower-level subgoals. An endpoint depending on its whole intervening action sequence is causally legitimate. It must not be counted as anticipatory unless the same latent is used to predict earlier primitive outcomes. The C88 criticism cannot be transferred to HWM from its use of action compression alone.
- [CoCo: Overcoming Statistical Bias in Action-Controllable World Models](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.04653v1) already trains temporal/spatial counterfactual consistency with inverse/zero-action and mirror relations. Those relations require their environment-specific assumptions. C88's oscillator has autonomous evolution; zero action does not mean static state, and reversing a command does not generally reverse dissipative dynamics. No such invalid consistency target is imposed in C88. This distinction alone is not a new research contribution.

Current contribution classification: developmental empirical falsification of one implementation/abstraction under an explicit intervention contract. Accepted novel method/problem claims: zero. If ordinary reindexing or native causal filtering suffices, stop this new-method route instead of naming a masking operation as an innovation.
