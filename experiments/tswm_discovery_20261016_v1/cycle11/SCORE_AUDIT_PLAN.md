# Frozen controlled score discriminator

Frozen before execution on2026-10-16. This is an autonomous diagnostic, not a reproduction of LENS, Branch-JEPA or Info-LDM, and not an accepted new contribution. LENS source inspection remains separate.

Question: can oracle conditional Energy Scores prefer a noninjective representation that removes predictable observable state information, even with equal exact frame-marginal distributions and equal latent dimension? Propriety concerns a fixed target; this study changes both encoded context and target. No result here refutes fixed-target propriety.

Process: fully observed2D state X with stationary N(0,I) marginal; X_next=Q*r*R(theta)*X+sqrt(1-r^2)*epsilon, independent standard normal epsilon and Q in{+1,-1} with P(Q=-1)=p. r=.9,theta=.3, p grid(0,.1,.25,.4,.5). Conditional law is a two-Gaussian mixture, not a Gaussian transition. No actions, hidden confounders, retrospective inputs, parameter fitting or future information. A sequence consists of independent stationary initial contexts and future innovations; within-sequence times are correlated.

Representations: identity and phi(X)=|X|*(cos(2angle X),sin(2angle X)), phi(0)=0. Both have exactly N(0,I) frame marginals and dimension2. phi(X)=phi(-X) discards an observable sign fiber. The fold is a valid Markov lumping for this process. Also compare frame-unit-normalized versions; both have identical uniform-circle frame marginals. Identical frame marginals do not imply identical future-block joint marginals.

Frozen grid: seeds2021,2022,2023;32768 iid stationary contexts per seed; horizons1 and8. Two independent future trajectories per context estimate the oracle Bayes Energy risk0.5*E(distance(Y,Y')) without training or finite-particle prediction approximation. Distance is Euclidean for H1 and RMS-over-time Euclidean block distance for H8(beta=1). Common innovations and uniform flip draws across p-values preserve paired comparisons; do not treat p-grid cells as independent replications. Report all grid cells, no cherry-picked threshold.

One-step exact density control: identity conditional density is (1-p)N(y;mu,sigma^2I)+pN(y;-mu,sigma^2I). Folded conditional density at phi(y) is0.5*(N(y;mu,sigma^2I)+N(y;-mu,sigma^2I)), because the fold has2inverse branches and Jacobian determinant2. This is independent of p. Both frame entropies equal the2D standard-Gaussian entropy, so their one-step oracle conditionalNLL difference equals the predictive mutual-information difference. This is an exact-existing likelihood/Info-LDM-principle control, not a native Info-LDM reproduction; its released implementation assumes fixed-covariance Gaussian prediction and its recovery theorem requires information saturation and stated exponential-family assumptions.

Raw observation prediction control: with the original context, the one-step raw conditional mean is(1-2p)*r*R(theta)*X. Given only phi(X), it is0. Therefore the extra Bayes raw-MSE due to folding is2*r^2*(1-2p)^2. p=.5 is a no-predictable-sign-information null. Also compare raw-space oracle Energy risks for full and folded contexts using independent uniform initial-sign draws. Averaged across stationary contexts the folded-context oracle risk is0.5*E(distance(rawY,rawY')) with two independent sign fibers.

Competing explanations: predictive-score bias across endogenous targets; approximation of a multimodal distribution; finite sample artifact; mismatch of representation scale/marginal; predictive information not actually relevant; insufficient latent dimension; or a full current method already grounding predictions in observed space. Exact kernels, identical marginals/dimensions, multiple independent context seeds, raw-space scores, exact density and explicit null discriminate them.

Boundary: Branch-JEPA v3 already trains decoded-space full-set Energy Score in addition to its latent score. Do not claim this diagnostic refutes its full objective or population theorem. Adding decoded scoring/reconstruction or using predictive information is an existing repair. Proper log-loss information principles and their data-processing motivation are old. Novelty remains unproven until closest work and actual model evidence show a residual beyond these controls. If the ordinary controls solve the only demonstrated problem, reject a new-method claim.
