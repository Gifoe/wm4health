The oracle construction in this cycle establishes a criterion-level distinction: lower Bayes score across different targets is not guaranteed to mean more predictive information. Current papers already limit their population guarantees to fixed targets or stated saturation/model assumptions. More importantly, all12 executed neural fits retain state in this task. This defeats the present empirical motivation for a new repair, even though the exact score example is real.

Possible rescue is a new theorem or a repeated failure in actual stochastic world-model training beyond existing information/decoded/EMA controls. That evidence is currently absent. Stop extending the toy; do not infer a top-conference gap merely from the construction.

Exploratory source triage now moves toward published nonlinear physical models with pretrained weights. Broad claims about memory, closure, nonstationarity or forced response remain crowded. A new task must compare current strong baselines and support an observable mechanism, not restate that forecasting accuracy differs from simulation fidelity. MDPI, secondary AI reviews and synthetic evaluation-only pages were excluded as evidence.
