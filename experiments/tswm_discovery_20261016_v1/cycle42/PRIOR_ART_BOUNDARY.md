# Prior-art boundary at the C42 screen

The primary-source C41 literature synthesis remains applicable. NCDSSM already separates auxiliary recognition/measurement evidence and continuous latent dynamics; modular NRI already reuses learned dynamics components; LIFTS already performs latent-driven joint inference/generation. An ordinary structured HMM/SSM already has the proposed observation × dynamics factorization. C42 does not relabel that factorization as an invention.

Newly checked on2026-10-17: Jelena Luketina, Jack Lanchantin, Sainbayar Sukhbaatar and Arthur Szlam, **Compositional Interfaces for Compositional Generalization**, CoLLAs2025, PMLR274:692–709. [Official primary page](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v274/luketina25a.html). The official abstract already reports modular observation encoders, a shared controller, unseen observation/action/instruction compositions and adaptation of new observation modules. This check is **abstract-level**; full PDF fetch through the web reader failed due its binary content type. No code or performance reproduction is claimed. Its control-policy setting differs from likelihood-based time-series simulation, but generic interchangeable-interface rhetoric is not new.

Neither a literature omission nor a gain over an entangled conditioner establishes a new world-model framework. The remaining development burden is a concrete useful interaction beyond this ordinary structured model, and evidence that it survives complete close baselines across multiple domains. C42 is an eligibility/ordinary-capability screen only.

Two additional primary-source checks, at abstract level only:

- [Meta-iLaD, ICML2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/ye26p.html): a few-shot meta-learned environment prior replaces pre-defined environment labels, with latent/environment/dynamics identifiability results under its assumptions. The old registry already screened its public repository without executable training code. This refresh checks the official proceedings abstract; it does not establish any sensor×system compositional failure. C42 supplies system IDs and therefore cannot be used as an unknown-environment adaptation result.
- [ViBR-WM, arXiv2609.33844v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33844): modular Bayesian regression combines visual features, physical histories and known covariates and accounts for parameter uncertainty/future disturbances. Its abstract reports four tasks; those results have not been reproduced here. This is another reason to retain ordinary probabilistic modeling as a complete contender and not define world models solely by latent-target losses or Transformer architectures.
