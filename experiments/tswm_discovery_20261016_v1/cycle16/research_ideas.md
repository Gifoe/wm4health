# Cycle16 candidate decisions

**Rejected as a new method:** repairing MTS3 cross-scale covariance with ordinary dense inference, or inventing specialized compression solely from the cycle15 witness. Full learned-model testing produced no NLL improvement, and the ordinary correction already has low measured inference cost. Further NP optimization would not establish a general world-model contribution.

**Still a question, not a recommended idea:** how to retain a useful, reusable belief over dynamics across conditional trajectory queries and newly acquired evidence. The active source channel is not enough evidence of an unresolved practical gap. Functional neural processes, latent-parameter models, recognition-parametrized beliefs and probabilistic meta-system identification already provide much of this capability.

The next useful discriminator is a full pretrained model with cross-system identification/simulation capabilities, rather than another manually specified covariance cell. Probabilistic meta-system-ID now has pinned official source, a verified public release checkpoint and a successful restricted native interface preflight. Its actual accuracy and information protocol still need evaluation; distinguish its marginal uncertainty contract from full joint simulation. If testing a residual, include ordinary correlated-residual postprocessing and coherent generative/functional baselines, and use independent held-out systems. Do not manufacture a gap by demanding an interface that a point-estimation paper does not claim.
