# Cycle16 negative evidence and rejected claims

1. **The module witness does not establish a trained undercoverage failure.** Cycle15's extreme admissible scalar case was real. After full native learning, nominal95% coverage is96.502% on this validation split; no analogous collapse is demonstrated.
2. **An ordinary inference replacement did not improve NLL.** Native0.231094, dense-joint0.240164. Keeping only dense fast covariance while resetting cross-scale dependence is worse on average at0.276096. This defeats a claim that the tested replacement alone is a useful new method. It does not prove retraining every corrected model useless.
3. **Context covariance is not unused.** Median trace contribution ratio0.358140; replacing covariance changes the readout. Reject the early-smoke interpretation that small/unused context necessarily explains the result. Spatial and cross-scale effects must be separated.
4. **Compensation is an explanation, not a demonstrated cause.** The variance decoder was trained under native moment approximations and may absorb them. Post-hoc covariance changes do not preserve its training distribution or identify the true output posterior.
5. **The model is a functioning pilot, not an optimally trained benchmark.** It improves substantially over initialization and is competitive with ordinary references here. One validation-selected seed and a fixed update budget cannot establish convergence, generalization across systems or stable superiority.
6. **Pre-fit adapter errors are not model failures.** Initial150-history alignment failed; source inspection fixed151-history before fitting. Native abstract action encoding uses all planned next-window inputs. Raw NP/PJM have two forecast covariates; prior six-channel prose was corrected separately from sealed cycle14 numbers. Future forecast issuance is unverified, hence calendar-only inputs here.
7. **Generic belief modeling is existing work.** Joint Gaussian inference, persistent posterior samples, functional NP/MPNP, recognition models, latent hypernetwork adaptation, probabilistic meta-system-ID and FIM-SDE rule out broad novelty claims. A new title or uncertainty token is not enough.

Current decision: stop the new covariance-compression/repair architecture line. Do not tune NP, repeat the same toy to obtain a large effect, or expand seeds/datasets without a distinct discriminator. The broader reusable-belief research question remains unresolved by this work; it is not an accepted idea.
