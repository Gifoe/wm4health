# Exploratory primary-source screening

Scores are provisional judgments of inspected evidence, not independent reproduction scores. Benchmark/theory papers receive N/A for experimental numeric scoring. Code execution is claimed only for selected ActSWM bodies.

- [ActSWM](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.26712v1) — pure method; arXiv2026 pinned preprint; venue acceptance not asserted. Insight/completeness/numeric: 3/3/3. Native source-default hinge and frozen inverse readout are the executed objects. The paper qualifies action separation by different consequences; the current task transfer does not refute its conditional theorem or reproduce Minecraft.
- [Keeping JEPA World Models Plannable](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.03137v1) — pure method; arXiv2026 pinned preprint; venue acceptance not asserted. Insight/completeness/numeric: 4/4/4. Learned inverse dynamics and encoder gradients are strong existing controls. The paper includes action-null/shuffle, simulator ranking and loss-weight controls; do not claim these diagnostics are absent.
- [MiraBench](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.29360v1) — pure benchmark; arXiv2026 pinned preprint; venue acceptance not asserted. Insight/completeness/numeric: 3/4/N/A benchmark/theory. Action effectiveness, failure preservation and optimistic simulation already motivate a benchmark. A generic ineffective-action benchmark is insufficient differentiation.
- [Counterfactual Action Evaluation, Observation Bottlenecks, and Representation Geometry in Joint-Embedding Predictive World Models](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.02860v1) — method + benchmark; arXiv2026 pinned preprint; venue acceptance not asserted. Insight/completeness/numeric: 4/4/4. Separates physical, observed, target-latent and predictor response. Public artifact permits checkpoint evaluation. Direct action-response auditing itself is covered.
- [The Intervention Gap in Latent World Models](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.29998v1) — method + benchmark; arXiv2026 pinned preprint; venue acceptance not asserted. Insight/completeness/numeric: 4/4/4. Capture-gated matched interventions separate readable state from propagated effects, with action-null, wrong-action, probe and support controls. The current pilot is narrower and fully observed.
- [DWM: Separating World Effects from Actions in Latent World Models](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.18715v1) — pure method; arXiv2026 pinned preprint; venue acceptance not asserted. Insight/completeness/numeric: 3/3/3. Already separates autonomous world effects and action effects and tests persistent-drift variants plus zero-action data. Null motion versus autonomous motion is not an untouched distinction.
- [CAER](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.30897v1) — pure method; arXiv2026 pinned preprint; venue acceptance not asserted. Insight/completeness/numeric: 3/3/3. Uses model-predicted action/null contrasts for training emphasis. Its predictive contrast is not claimed to identify causal graphs. Model self-contrast may merit calibration checks, but this pilot did not execute CAER.
- [FLEX-WAM](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.05483v1) — pure method; arXiv2026 pinned preprint; venue acceptance not asserted. Insight/completeness/numeric: 3/3/3. Action/state sensitivity balance uses a model-relative elasticity target. It differs from a universal positive separation margin and was not executed here.
- [Sensorimotor](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.20104v1) — pure method; arXiv2026 pinned preprint; venue acceptance not asserted. Insight/completeness/numeric: 3/3/3. Inverse conditional information, state/action structure and homomorphism foundations are relevant existing mechanisms. Abstract and method were screened; no implementation or numerical claim is verified by this cycle.
- [State/action homomorphisms, IJCAI 2003](https://www.ijcai.org/Proceedings/03/Papers/145.pdf) — theory/proof; IJCAI2003 proceedings. Insight/completeness/numeric: 4/3/N/A benchmark/theory. State-dependent equivalent actions and action abstraction are old concepts. A useful residual must concern estimation or generalization under a precise modern training protocol.
- [DoTime](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.27263v1) — method + benchmark; arXiv2026 pinned preprint; venue acceptance not asserted. Insight/completeness/numeric: 4/4/3. Temporal intervention generator, versioned suites and native protocol inspected. Its public HF card reports the observational-target erratum: the original direction advantage does not survive correction. No PFN weight execution in this cycle.
- [CausalLongPFN](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.05797v1) — pure method; arXiv2026 pinned preprint; venue acceptance not asserted. Insight/completeness/numeric: 3/3/3. Existing planned longitudinal-treatment PFN baseline. Paper screening only; no checkpoint inference or reproduction claimed.
- [Amortizing Causal Sensitivity Analysis with Prior-Fitted Networks](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.10590v1) — pure method; arXiv2026 pinned preprint; venue acceptance not asserted. Insight/completeness/numeric: 4/3/3. Amortized sensitivity bounds already exist for static treatment tasks. A generic partial-identification PFN is not new; temporal distinctions need comparison with longitudinal sensitivity literature.
- [Counterfactual Debugging of World Model Transfer](https://mingxuan.me/assets/papers/ctf_debugging.pdf) — method + benchmark; author-hosted primary PDF; acceptance not independently used. Insight/completeness/numeric: 4/4/4. Mechanism replacement and Shapley attribution use queryable world/environment mechanisms with explicit sparsity and interaction assumptions. No source execution here; do not equate its independent world noise with shared-noise counterfactual coupling.


## Closest-work clusters and residuals

Action-discriminative training: ActSWM, SLIM, Sensorimotor, FLEX-WAM. Covered: inverse/action information and sensitivity repair. Under-tested in this cycle: a validated learned encoder under conditionally equivalent actions. Possible differentiation: finite-data effect calibration under state aliasing, only if existing probabilistic inverse controls fail.

Physical fidelity and autonomous effects: MiraBench, Counterfactual Action Evaluation, Intervention Gap, DWM, CAER. Covered: failure preservation, physical-vs-latent effects, capture gates, action/null contrasts and autonomous dynamics. Residual candidate: objective-level attribution under an exactly specified task; not itself a new benchmark or new method.

Causal amortization: DoTime, CausalLongPFN, CSA-PFN. Covered: interventional priors, longitudinal planned treatments and amortized sensitivity bounds. Public erratum is an essential protocol anchor. A temporal identified-set problem must be distinguished from forecast quantiles and existing longitudinal sensitivity theory before implementation.

No closest-paper title is a kill gate. The rejection here concerns overly broad novelty claims and the already-successful ordinary control; precise unexplored residuals remain hypotheses.
