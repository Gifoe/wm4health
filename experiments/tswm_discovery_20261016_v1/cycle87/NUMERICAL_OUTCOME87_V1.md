The environment reports float32 matmul precision `high`, TF32 enabled. A static diagnostic finds batch-size-dependent field differences up to.00507, with exact repeated outputs at fixed inputs and zero change from replacing other query rows by zeros at the same batch size. PyTorch documents that batch/slice computations need not be bitwise equivalent: https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.8/notes/numerical_accuracy.html . This is an implementation/numerical issue to disclose, not a newly established scientific research gap or proof that TF32 alone caused the trajectory discrepancy.

We stop extending precision probes in this low-priority sampler-method route. The failed criterion remains failed; it is not relaxed to manufacture a passing certificate. Reported simulation metrics retain their fixed RK4/TF32 protocol and should not be interpreted as fully certified continuous-time errors. The54/54 ordinary-control comparisons describe that protocol.
