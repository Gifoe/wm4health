# Scope retained from the user's correction

The deliverable is a reusable **time-series world-model framework**, at the problem level exemplified by WorldTS. A dataset-specific accuracy improvement, a sampler wrapper, an implementation repair, or a new name for an ordinary estimator does not meet the goal. The concrete idea remains an autonomous research decision, conditional on literature and falsification evidence.

Candidate mechanisms must specify what state/evolution capability is learned, how it is reused, what future reasoning it enables, and why the strongest existing complete methods are insufficient. A known-component combination is eligible if its necessary interaction and useful learning/generalization effect are established; no arbitrary requirement for a new mathematical operator is imposed.

The current C44 known-process experiment is an **eligibility/negative-control screen** for one reserve route, not the proposed framework or a domain-specific tuning project. Its functions and carriers expose model bias and query cost under controlled truth. Passing it would still require a concrete new mechanism, stronger baseline comparisons, independent domains, and held-out tasks. Failing it should end that particular claim rather than invite repeated tuning on the same carriers.

The observation/dynamics uncertainty-interface route remains separate. No final research direction has been accepted. The overall goal is ACTIVE and UNACHIEVED.
