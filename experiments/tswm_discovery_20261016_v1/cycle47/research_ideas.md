# C47 idea triage

Research unit remains a reusable time-series world-model framework at WorldTS scope. Dataset-specific score optimization is not the goal; a framework may use known components if their distinct interaction has independent value. Generic state-space-model expressivity alone is not a veto.

Current tested recipe: analytically integrate conditionally Poisson observations and match joint clock/mark transforms alongside NLL. Deprioritize the current fixed-feature Laplace / hybrid recipe as a new world-model framework. In this qualified screen it does not improve complete-clock likelihood training: the bistable future-energy differences are adverse in both training seeds, and OU provides no reliable future-quality advantage. The mean-clock restriction loses useful dependence, but a complete ordinary stochastic-clock model already restores it.

Do not recommend this as one of the final directions. No second or third final direction is invented. An efficiency variant remains an unselected lead, with matched-time and scalability evidence missing and substantial transform/signature prior art.

Next development must start from a consequential capability and strong actual neural world-model implementations on independent domains. Repeating these toy carriers, enlarging a feature bank after EVAL, or renaming ordinary joint likelihood would provide little new information.
