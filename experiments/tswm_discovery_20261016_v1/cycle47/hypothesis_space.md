# C47 competing explanations

H0: complete stochastic-clock likelihood already captures the useful dependence. Supported within this finite-grid screen by the reference and future-law comparisons.

H1: conditioning the observation clock only on a deterministic mean removes useful state-innovation dependence. The restricted arm has adverse NLL and future ES in both carriers; independent count/state covariance confirms its zero conditional innovation coupling. This is an ablation result, not a universal failure of HyperNSDE.

H2: the proposed fixed Laplace bank adds necessary learning signal beyond full NLL. Not supported by the frozen future-law comparison; the bistable seed effects are adverse. No extrapolation to all feature banks.

H3: apparent loss quality reflects inference/gradient/forecast-boundary errors. Direct Poisson Monte Carlo, finite differences, independent log filtering, boundary moments and full independent EVAL arithmetic pass. Remaining finite-grid/known-emission restrictions still apply.

H4: the method offers a useful equal-cost advantage at scale. Unresolved, not established by lower per-step time; requires fresh checkpoint/compute protocol and unknown-emission continuous or real-domain evidence. Current evidence does not justify expensive expansion to force a positive result.

H5: adverse feature loss is due to finite-bank information, weights, objective bias or finite optimization. Still competing; diagnosing them might explain this method, but is not itself evidence of a new general world-model problem.
