# C64: Frozen observational trajectory laws under policy removal

Research question, not accepted final direction: can a frozen probabilistic block forecaster be reused for interventions after removing logged feedback-policy factors, and what remains beyond ordinary causal models and established samplers? The underlying conditioning/causal-factorization issue is known (C29). CaTSG covers a static backdoor setup and explicitly limits feedback/time-varying confounding; DiffPO already uses propensity weighting in training. Generic guidance, inverse propensity or SMC are not claimed novel. This cycle is an exact/learned Gaussian attribution stage, not a new diffusion method or real-world result. A nonlinear learned-model and independent-domain stage would be required before a framework claim.

System: Y_t=.8Y_(t-1)+.7U_t+.5epsilon_t; logged U_t=-kY_(t-1)+.5eta_t, independent standard Normal innovations, Y0=.25. Gains k={0,.25,.75,2}; every logged closed-loop coefficient .8-.7k has absolute value below1. All states are observed, actions precede next innovation, and Gaussian policy has full support. No hidden confounding; no uniform positive density lower bound is asserted. Horizons1/4/16/64; fixed queried plans zero and .4sin(2pi(t+1)/8). The query plan is an intervention, not merely a conditional event.

Removing the known policy factor is mathematically ordinary causal factorization, not a proposed innovation.
