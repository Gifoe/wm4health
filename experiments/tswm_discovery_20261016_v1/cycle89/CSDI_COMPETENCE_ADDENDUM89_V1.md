# Prospective numerical interpretation, before the pilot fit

For the existing protocol, define a large off-manifold residual as a family-average ratio above .5: for each query, the RMS distance of each generated future trajectory to its nearest exact mechanism trajectory is averaged over samples and divided by the RMS spread of the exact mixture around its posterior mean. All distances use the same TRAIN state standardization and the same complete 2x64 future vector. Report the complete values, not just a pass label. This is a screening criterion, not a physical theorem or a calibrated mechanism-identification test. A generator must also improve the trajectory energy score over the exact deterministic posterior mean in each family. Both checks are declared before the training process starts. Failed EVAL screening prohibits tuning this pilot on EVAL.

Use the unbiased U-statistic for the generated-sample pair term in energy score and scalar terminal CRPS, exact prior-weighted reference trajectories for the outcome term, and paired bootstrap over the eight independent four-plan query groups per family (10000 draws, seed20268918) for descriptive uncertainty. One optimizer seed cannot establish training robustness.
