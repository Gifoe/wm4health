# Ordinary learned persistent-latent world-model control

Frozen after the CSDI pilot competence screen and before this control is fitted. The old CSDI EVAL is not reused to choose this model's degree, component count, ridge or stopping point. A fresh EVAL query seed20268923 is used for both the already frozen CSDI checkpoint and this control. The purpose is falsification of a new-architecture claim, not rescuing a preferred method.

Fit a standard finite mixture of polynomial state-transition regressions, separately for each of the two known family indicators. Nine persistent mixture components are known in advance from the controlled benchmark specification; ground-truth mechanism labels/parameters are never read by fitting. State observations x,z are fully observed during TRAIN; control u is exogenous. Each latent component has degree5 polynomial features of [previous x,previous z,current u], including all monomials up to total degree5 (56 terms), predicting the two standardized state increments. Feature RMS and state scale use TRAIN only. The component is held fixed throughout an entire 64-step rollout. Its mixing law is independent of future action queries. This is a conventional mixture of regressions, not a proposed new mechanism or a native-paper reproduction. It has a strong low-dimensional Markov/polynomial inductive bias and known component count; do not call it information/capacity matched to CSDI or broad evidence about partially observed worlds.

Initialize responsibilities by ordinary9-means of per-trajectory linear-transition coefficients, using observed TRAIN states/actions only. Use deterministic NumPy/SciPy seed20268924,20 ordinary kmeans starts selected by TRAIN inertia. Run exactly40 EM iterations. Weighted least squares ridge1e-6 times the effective transition count, per-component isotropic residual variance floor1e-7, mixture probability floor1e-6. Checkpoints after iterations1/5/10/20/40; select by CAL full-trajectory mixture negative log likelihood. Forecasts use deterministic component mean transitions, with one sampled persistent component; residual fitting variance is not claimed to represent a real process-noise source.

Generate the same64 action-query format using fresh seed20268923, eight groups of four plans per family, and the exact nine-world oracle bank. Neither this EVAL nor the previous CSDI EVAL may select mixture hyperparameters/iteration. Evaluate the exact nine-atom learned forecast distribution, with no Monte Carlo error for the mixture. Use trajectory energy score and the same off-manifold screening threshold as CSDI. A frozen CSDI checkpoint may be evaluated on the fresh queries with128 samples each; no retraining. Report learned mixture marginal calibration, physical residual and whether shared-component generation is consistent across different plans. Shared-component sampling preserves each learned per-action marginal exactly for every query-list size by construction; that elementary property is an existing latent-generative-model control, not innovation. True physical-mechanism agreement must still be measured independently and must not be inferred from matching latent indices.

The model's decision is not whether it wins a leaderboard. If an ordinary persistent-latent generator learns calibrated, physically supported multi-action distributions, the controlled setting does not establish a need for a new coupling architecture. If it fails, report capacity/optimization/inductive-bias limitations before treating any failure as a research gap. One seed and two small controlled systems cannot establish a final publishable direction.
