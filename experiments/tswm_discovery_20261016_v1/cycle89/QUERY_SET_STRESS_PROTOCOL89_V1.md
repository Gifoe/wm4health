# C89B: compatibility is not automatically a calibrated coupling

Working question, not accepted novelty: when hypothetical action branches are coordinated to share a mechanism, are the original per-action mechanism probabilities preserved as the number of plans changes? A query list supplies no new real-world evidence. OneWorld already derives a prior-corrected exact evidence ratio that **does** recover a proper shared-world joint law under its assumptions; do not claim its formula necessarily distorts marginals. Its actual learned compatibility interpreter is trained on same-world versus mismatched-world labels. A source-inspired terminal score stress can separate that relational objective from a density-ratio objective, but is not a reproduction of native OneWorld flow guidance or its reported benchmark results.

This first stress deliberately grants the critic exact mechanism identity, to remove perception/identification error as a possible explanation. It is a **privileged finite-world diagnostic**, not a learned world-model comparison and not evidence of a defect in a released checkpoint. Any positive research claim still needs actual trained stochastic time-series generators and closest-work differentiation.

## Fixed setup before fitting critics

Nine persistent worlds, prior [.4,.2,.1,.08,.07,.05,.04,.035,.025]. Group sizes K=2,3,4, all seen during training. Gaussian interpreter follows the prior-corrected diagonal-Gaussian product integral, latent dimension32, with global learned Gaussian prior and per-world additive natural-parameter evidence (positive precision increments). The integral is computed analytically, rather than with the64-sample importance approximation used in OneWorld. Mechanism IDs are oracle inputs to a lookup encoder; this is explicitly a qualification upper bound, not a claim of source architecture/configuration reproduction.

Three ordinary critic arms: (A) Gaussian product score trained on shared-world positives versus IID-prior negative groups **conditioned on all identities being distinct**; (B) same Gaussian score trained against fully independent-prior negatives, including compatible collisions; (C) conventional DeepSets/count MLP trained on the independent-negative objective as a stronger flexible density-ratio comparator. A/B are capacity/initialization matched; C is not capacity matched and is not a proposed method.

Seeds2026896/2026897/2026898,10000 Adam updates per arm, lr=.01, batch256, CPU two threads, gradient clip10. Same-world positives sample one world from the declared prior and repeat it. Both classes have probability.5. K is uniform among2/3/4. Negative rejection sampling uses a separate RNG so it cannot alter positive groups or K/label draws. Select among updates0/1000/3000/6000/10000 by the exact balanced population classification BCE over **all9^K tuples**, averaged over K. Compare with the exact Bayes classification lower bound for each negative law. No recoupled marginal, coherence or query-count metric participates in selection. This finite-population study makes no independent-system generalization claim.

## Measurements after selection

Enumerate the exact independent-world proposal probability for every tuple and reweight by exp(gamma*score), gamma in [0,.01,.1,1], normalized in log space. This is terminal Gibbs recoupling, **not** the original continuous-time flow guidance. Report per-branch total variation from the original prior, all-branches-same probability, and the anchor branch's marginal change across K. Keep source classification competence separate from coupling calibration.

Ordinary exact controls: independence; conditioning only on all identities agreeing (weights proportional to prior^K); and the exact Bayesian shared/independent density ratio (correct marginals for every K). A complete-support marginal-constrained coupling is a possible ordinary feasibility comparator; no optimal-transport/ensemble-reordering primitive may be renamed an innovation. Properly calibrated likelihood-ratio guidance can preserve the marginals: that is a falsification control, not a competing result to hide.

Interpretation: a high-performing compatibility classifier with distorted recoupled probabilities supports an **objective/measurement concern in this finite oracle setting**. It does not prove a native model failure or a new research gap. If exact-ratio or known marginal-preserving methods cover the intended setting, narrow the contribution honestly; do not add complexity to evade a strong baseline. PAWBench already studies repeated-future calibration, OneWorld studies shared-physics branches, and EDDY/Couple-to-Control already study marginal-preserving sample interactions/noise. The possible remaining angle is their intersection for distinct hypothetical action queries; novelty remains unverified until exact scopes and actual learned generators are tested.
