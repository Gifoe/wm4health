## Qualification before a learned-model campaign

The proposed single-physical-parameter fit can reject a correct posterior predictive mean if the context does not identify the mechanism. It is therefore unsafe as a general world-model criterion. Before training or criticizing a paper, test an exact Bayesian oracle whose history is deliberately uninformative about a persistent parameter. Keep the parameter fixed within each simulated world; mix complete trajectories with one shared posterior across all queries. A mixture-aware necessary condition must accept this oracle. A deliberately query-dependent world switch is a negative control.

This is a supporting measurement qualification, not a learned-world-model reproduction, a novel-method experiment, or completion of the user objective. It must not be used as a substitute for actual trained models. Parameter labels and known equations are privileged diagnostics only. Mean compatibility is necessary, not sufficient for coherent joint predictive distributions or identifiable cross-world counterfactual coupling.
