# Negative evidence

A large TTC can be induced by a latent-origin shift while unchanged learned weights and algebraic wrappers yield exactly unchanged decoded predictions in both native checkpoints. Time-permuted latent states also reach near-one TTC at the largest fixed offset. These facts reject TTC alone as a certificate of learned temporal behaviour. They do not reject the predictive usefulness of the two models, nor provide a new world-model mechanism. The metric issue is known mathematics and does not satisfy novelty.
