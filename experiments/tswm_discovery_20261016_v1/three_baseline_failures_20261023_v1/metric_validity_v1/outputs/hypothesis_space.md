# Competing interpretations

H1: higher absolute temporal cosine certifies more faithful temporal dynamics. Unsupported as a standalone interpretation: physical predictions remain identical across a wide range of scores, and shuffled states can score highly. H2: cosine also measures common latent offsets. Supported by the frozen offset experiment. H3: an origin-invariant scalar alone measures behavioural faithfulness. Untested and not assumed; centering is just an existing control. H4: the published geometry numbers are erroneous. Not tested or claimed.
