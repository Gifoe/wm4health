# Competing hypotheses

Execution/numeric drift is contradicted by byte-identical seed42 weights/predictions and independent metrics. Initialization explains part of H336 variation but does not alone close the three tested H720 cases. The proposed sparse-to-hourly degradation is falsified. Different early stopping/update counts remain a concrete alternative to representation/loss incompatibility; the fixed100 matrix tests it. AE pretraining lineage and objective/model-class differences remain separate explanations. No single cause is accepted before that comparison.
