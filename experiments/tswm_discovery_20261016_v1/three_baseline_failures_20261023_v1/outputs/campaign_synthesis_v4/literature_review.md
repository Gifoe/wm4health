# Primary work and novelty boundary

Primary: [WorldTS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31162), [LatentTSF v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.00297v2), [TSWM accuracy/mechanism benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01842). Their input and supervision protocols differ. Existing nearest-work screening remains [V2](../../NOVELTY_SCREEN_20261023_V2.md). Temporal-cosine origin sensitivity is not novel; see also [representation invariance, ICML2019](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/kornblith19a.html). These checks do not establish an accepted new direction.
