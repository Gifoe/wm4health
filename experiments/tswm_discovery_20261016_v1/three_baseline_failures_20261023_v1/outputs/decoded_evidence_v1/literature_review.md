# Novelty screen after a real failure, before a method claim

The fixed100 latent/raw gap justifies an attribution experiment. It does not make the broad question “good reconstruction can still produce poor dynamics” novel. Primary-source checks on2026-10-23 directly constrain that claim:

| Primary source | Relevant existing coverage | Consequence here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [LatentTSF v2, May12](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.00297v2) | Section5.3 already compares decoded-space supervision; further sections study dynamical structure and encoder sensitivity. The appendix retains the Electricity/DLinear references0.185 and0.218 used by our ledger. | Existing decoded-loss flags are an attribution control. Neither changing their weights nor restating the frozen-encoder limitation is a new contribution. The paper-reference version check does not eliminate the recorded H720 discrepancy. |
| [Beyond Compression, Sep24](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.30198v1) | Studies reconstruction-oriented latent states versus long autoregressive stability; examines decoder sensitivity and training interventions including dynamics constraints, perturbations and recursive training. | Generic reconstruction-versus-rollout mismatch and decoder-sensitivity remedies have direct prior art. Our direct forecasting discrepancy does not independently establish an autoregressive rollout defect. |
| [PDE-JEPA, Sep28](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34715v1) | Studies predictive representations for parametric PDEs; uses a latent geometry projector and a structured predictor to connect representations to physical evolution and parameter response. | Generic representation–dynamics alignment and geometry projection already have close competition. A later general time-series claim would need a distinct mechanism/setting and comparable existing controls. |

These two new papers are **prior-art checks**, not fourth/fifth primary reproduction campaigns. We have read their primary sources; we have not reproduced their headline results, verified their advertised effect sizes, or established that they solve every action-conditioned, stochastic or partially observed time-series problem.

Decision: retract any suggestion that the broad state/transition-compatibility phrase itself is a new research direction. Continue the already registered ordinary decoded-loss attribution matrix, and use its Validation-only branch for any predictor-capacity follow-up. No novel model is being validated. A specific residual could still matter, but needs evidence and a narrower defensible novelty boundary.

No Test hyperparameter is selected from this literature check. Existing failures and false hypotheses remain in the ledger. The full research goal stays active; accepted final novel directions remain0.
