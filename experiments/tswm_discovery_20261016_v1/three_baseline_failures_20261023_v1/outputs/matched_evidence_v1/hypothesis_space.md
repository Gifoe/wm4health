Seed42 numeric/execution drift is contradicted by byte-identical replay. Sparse-hour degradation is falsified. Fixed100 finding: 固定100epoch后，raw在六个配对单元中仍全部更好。早停确实影响绝对结果，但“仅因为raw训练更多轮”不足以解释已观察到的完整差距。残差属于发布配方级差距，尚未唯一归因于AE、latent loss或可学习函数类。

Distinct remaining hypotheses: released AE lineage/reconstruction bias; changed function class under nonlinear representation; latent surrogate objective mismatch. Separate controls and independent mechanisms are required; no unique representation-information-loss attribution is accepted.
