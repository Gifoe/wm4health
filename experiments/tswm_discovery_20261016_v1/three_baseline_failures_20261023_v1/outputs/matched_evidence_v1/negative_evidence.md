# Negative evidence

Dense hourly inference improved the six original latent models. Runtime incidents are not model deficiencies. Fixed100 finding: 固定100epoch后，raw在六个配对单元中仍全部更好。早停确实影响绝对结果，但“仅因为raw训练更多轮”不足以解释已观察到的完整差距。残差属于发布配方级差距，尚未唯一归因于AE、latent loss或可学习函数类。

No independent new-method benefit or general world-model gap has been established. Ordinary decoded losses and expressive predictors remain mandatory explanations/controls.
