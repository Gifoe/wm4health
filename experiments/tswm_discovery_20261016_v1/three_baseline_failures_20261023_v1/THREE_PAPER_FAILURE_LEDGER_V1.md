# Three-paper failures: what deserves further work?

The main problem in the prior campaign was breadth without a settled failure attribution: many different models, datasets and mechanisms were tried, while no final research direction passed. Another unconstrained idea list would repeat that mistake. This campaign keeps three primary papers and follows one actual failure at a time.

| Paper | Real existing execution | Observed failure / negative evidence | Current decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WorldTS | Four original NP/PJM baseline cells; subsequent Loop, readout, Stage-1 and NESO studies | Loop gains were not consistent across tasks/seeds; low readout-gradient cosine did not prove a conflict; the E1 validation minimum did not establish lost state information; an ordinary six-coefficient correction explained much of the NESO updater gain | Do not reopen these old architectures. A new claim must address a distinct unclosed mechanism, rather than rename the same loss/updater. |
| LatentTSF | Sixteen original baseline cells, six current source-recipe repeats, official raw-mode controls and registered fixed-cap follow-up | Source-recipe DLinear/Electricity H336/H720 did not meet the earlier numerical reproduction band; all six raw controls performed better. Dense hourly queries improved all six latent models, so the proposed workload degradation is false. Early stopping required an explicit attribution control. | Current focused case. Consult the fixed100 terminal report before attributing a representation defect. A score discrepancy alone is insufficient novelty. |
| TSWM recipe | Forty C53 source-based subset fits across two backbones, four arms and five seeds; later temporal audit | The existing temporal audit did not find the anticipated future-action leakage. Accuracy-versus-directional-consistency divergence and directional supervision are already in the paper. | Preserve the negative result. Neither a rediscovery of the published gap nor a changed information protocol constitutes a new world-model idea. |

Execution counts above are not full paper reproductions. TSWM is a benchmark and training recipe, rather than one unique third network. WorldTS/TSWM have conditional future inputs; history-only LatentTSF is not placed in the same unqualified ranking. The current code/weight rehash audit validates identities, not a fresh execution of every old fit.

The fixed-cap result is intentionally separate from the native recipe: [registered protocol](MATCHED_CAP_PROTOCOL_V1.md), [terminal report](outputs/matched_evidence_v1/experimental_results.md). Its controls match epochs and minibatch counts, not nonlinear function classes, exact initial tensors or FLOPs. A surviving gap still needs discrimination between AE provenance, representation/predictor compatibility and objective mismatch.

The failure-derived candidate is **when a reconstruction-trained state is suitable for reusable dynamics learning**. It has an experimental handle but has not yet passed novelty, independent-system importance or method-benefit tests. A useful contribution could combine existing ingredients in a substantively new way; it does not require an unprecedented mathematical primitive. It does require a residual and independent benefit beyond the ordinary controls that already explain earlier cases.
