# Failure-derived questions, not architecture proposals

Current concrete question: is the released latent forecasting recipe's apparent long-horizon discrepancy stable across native initialization and realistic forecast-origin schedules, or explained by protocol/selection variability?

This is a falsifiable diagnostic question, not yet a top-venue research direction. The next method question is deliberately left open until the observed outcomes and strongest ordinary explanations are available. No Loop, Jacobian loss, special calibration module or novel name is introduced merely to populate an idea list.

If a real, repeatable state/dynamics deficit emerges, formulate its exact information/compute contract and at least two competing mechanisms, then search prior art and test the cheapest distinguishing intervention on independent development data. Ordinary success under the same contract withdraws that particular new-method motivation.
