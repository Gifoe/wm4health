# Additional primary-paper screening

These readings occurred after the main literature ledger was written. They do
not establish a new direction or an executed baseline.

- [Shared Gaussianization,2610.10299v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.10299v1):
  the abstract/method structure already covers nuisance preference, explicit
  alignment weighting, finite-batch bias, and an unbiased U-statistic. A generic
  "Gaussian regularization retains nuisance" proposal is not novel. Full
  independent theorem/code verification was not completed.
- [A Control Theory of Predictability,2607.10362v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.10362v1):
  distinguishes latent metric and off-manifold extrapolation contributions;
  its stated empirical scope includes a Jacobian proxy and synthetic known
  operators. Merely showing data-averaged prediction error fails to certify
  planner-chosen trajectories is already covered. The listed limitations do
  not by themselves justify a new safety-monitor method.
- [What Must a World Model Distinguish for Planning?,2609.33030v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33030v1):
  distinguishes candidate discovery from selection and studies query placement,
  compact representations and held-out objective compositions. Generic
  decision-sufficiency compression or separating goal-conditioned proposals
  from physical prediction is covered. Relevant method/appendix sections were
  read; no independent source execution or numerical reproduction is claimed.

Further experiments need a precise residual after known probabilistic,
contrastive/predictive-state and causal-sequence controls. A search result title
or an author's future-work paragraph is not proof that such a residual exists.
