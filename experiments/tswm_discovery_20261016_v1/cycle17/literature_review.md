# Closest-work boundary update

Broad joint uncertainty, efficient updates and simulation fidelity are established research areas; the present gate does not justify renaming them as new.

- [incTNP](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/mortimer26a.html), ICML2026; pinned2602.18955v2. Causal caching, incremental evidence updates and an implicit Bayesianness metric already cover the generic update story. Its static regression streams do not themselves settle action-conditioned dynamical reuse.
- [Efficient Autoregressive Inference for Transformer Probabilistic Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09477v2), ICLR2026 camera-ready per primary record. Causal buffer separates cached context from generated targets; joint prediction plus caching alone is not a new method. Full dynamics mechanisms were not tested here.
- [Diffusion Sequence Models for Generative In-Context Meta-Learning of Robot Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13366v1), preprint. Generative trajectory meta-models and OOD robot dynamics are direct controls for a proposed joint world model. Claimed performance is not independently reproduced by this project.
- [Dynamic-Probabilistic Consistency Gap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31547v1), preprint. Explicitly distinguishes predictive probabilistic objectives from autonomous dynamical fidelity and uses filtered innovations/Jacobian transport. A generic "good NLL, wrong dynamics" claim is already occupied. Partial observability or nonstationarity could be different settings, but no new gap is inferred from their absence.

HTML/reading files and hashes are saved in outputs/literature. The blocked OpenReview PDF was not bypassed; the public arXiv camera-ready version was used instead. Literature is evidence of coverage, not proof every residual problem is solved. Any next candidate needs a specific difference and direct evidence beyond these primitives.
