## Generated systems and splits

Two deterministic SISO families, explicitly defined here, with fully available scalar output history and known exogenous future control. No clinical causal claim or feedback confounding. Let a1=2*r*cos(theta), a2=-r*r, b=k*(1-a1-a2). The recurrence is y[t+1]=a1*y[t]+a2*y[t-1]+b*u[t]-c*y[t]^3. Linear c=0; nonlinear c is uniform [0.0002,0.0010]. Draw r uniform [.94,.98], theta [.08,.18], k [.7,1.3]. Controls are uniform [-1,1] held for16 steps, independent of states. Initial state [0,0], burn-in1024, retain3300 response samples and inputs. Exact alignment: y[t] precedes u[t], which affects y[t+1]. Reject no generated system based on model scores. Nonfinite or |y|>100 generation fails the entire qualification rather than silently excluding it.

Context L=3200, horizon H=100. Six independent system/forcing seeds per family for VAL and six per family for EVAL (24 systems total), seeds 680100+1000*subset_index+100*family_index+i, with subset index0/1 and family index0/1. Parameters, initial states and all arrays remain on server G. Known future input supplied to conditional models is u[L-1:L+H-1], the causal input for each next response; historical aligned input is u[-1] (zero before burn-in) shifted by one relative to y. Thus channels [y[t], u[t-1]] have direct same-time response/control meaning. All models receive the identical aligned channels. Exact source recurrence and shifted arrays independently replayed.
