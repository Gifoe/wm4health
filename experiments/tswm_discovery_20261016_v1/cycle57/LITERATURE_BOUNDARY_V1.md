# C57 prior-art boundary and benchmark scope

[RP-GSSM](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/hromadka26a.html) supplies the auxiliary objective and conjugate smoother. The unmodified source is pinned at a75225d5edceb972bb872dc9a335608733567dd4. C57 changes the controlled-prior family and execution backend and therefore reports an adapted native-loss mechanism experiment, not a full paper reproduction.

[SogCLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/yuan22b.html) already studies global contrastive optimization with small batches, and the [NeurIPS gradient-bias study](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/db174d373133dcc6bf83bc98e4b681f8-Abstract-Conference.html) already studies minibatch gradient bias and an augmentation remedy. The inspected [SogCLR source](https://github.com/Optimization-AI/SogCLR/blob/ef289b52ed72d22d8b3c4e8a76d698c117f02a44/sogclr/builder.py) uses normalized representations and detached running-denominator weights. A direct Gaussian-kernel adaptation does not inherit the original pipeline's bounded-similarity behavior. Its failed unshifted attempt is retained; exact algebraic centering and running means are not claimed innovations.

[Normalizing Kalman Filters](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2020/file/1f47cef5e38c952f94c5d61726027439-Paper.pdf) already combines invertible observation transformations with Gaussian filtering and exact likelihood. The C57 proper control is a small parametric instance of this principle. It does not implement the authors' full architecture and must not be presented as a new reconstruction-free world model.

The current [Pythia report](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05240v1) explicitly studies context-conditioned predictive representations for observational forecasting. Its two-stage representation/readout recipe is prior art. Lack of an intervention result in that report is not evidence that an intervention method was tested and failed. No Pythia fit or checkpoint is executed in C57.

[EpiCF-Bench](https://github.com/complex-ai-lab/epi-cf-benchmark) already supplies trajectory, distributional, interval, calibration and effect evaluation utilities; [TIME](https://github.com/zqiao11/TIME) supports quantile forecasting evaluation, while [DoTime](https://github.com/thummd/dotime) supplies controlled interventional generators with version-sensitive counterfactual pairing. These repositories defeat a claim that such evaluation is wholly absent. They are not C57 training data or executed C57 baselines. Any later transfer must pin versions, examine identifiability and use the relevant joint or marginal target rather than infer capability from repository titles.

Novelty remains unestablished. C57 asks whether an observed defect requires a new mechanism after competent existing controls. Two paired synthetic sensors alone cannot establish a broad unresolved world-model problem or validate a final research direction.
