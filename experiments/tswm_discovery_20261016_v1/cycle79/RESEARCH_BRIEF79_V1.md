This is a new multi-perspective synthesis after the C75-C78 scalar-sensor lead was retired. Prior searches/experiments remain visible; this freeze is before the new delegated scouting, not before the entire project history. Scope: general learned time-series world models, including autonomous and action-conditioned state/transition learning across systems. Main recency window2026-04-18 through2026-10-18; follow older foundational work when needed. Target reader: the researcher deciding what can withstand a strong methodological review and a single RTX5090 validation.

RQ1: Which general world-model capabilities are actually claimed and tested by current methods, under which input, training, state-observation, action and distribution-shift contracts? Which failures have direct independent or reproducible evidence, rather than a low forecasting score or a paper's omission?

RQ2: Which candidate failure mechanisms remain after implementation/data/selection defects, inadequate training, ordinary identification/filtering/causal/state-space controls and incompatible task definitions are excluded? What do competing methods already solve, and under exactly which assumptions do their results stop applying?

RQ3: What specific unresolved mechanism question is both distinguishable from established work and falsifiable by a minimal executable experiment on one RTX5090, with strong same-information baselines and independent held-out evidence? No idea must be proposed if evidence is insufficient.

Angle: compare evidence about general capabilities and surviving causal explanations, rather than collect model names or promote another dataset-specific deficit. Every usable reference needs verified title/authors/year/version/primary URL and claim-level support. Metadata-only sources cannot support numeric, theorem or code claims. Author-reported results remain separate from our execution.
