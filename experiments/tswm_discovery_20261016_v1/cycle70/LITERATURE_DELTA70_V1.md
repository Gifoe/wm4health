# Scope and novelty boundary

[Meta-iLaD, ICML2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/ye26p.html), main text sections3-4, already learns a Gaussian environment prior from distinct same-environment context trajectories and evolves a NODE from an inferred initial state. Its environment identifiability result assumes an injective trajectory map, regular sufficient statistics and full-rank environment variability; state identifiability adds conditional-factorization and derivative-variability conditions. These are population results, not a guarantee that arbitrary short histories identify every system. AppendixE uses preceding windows under local stationarity and explicitly notes incorrect context/query pairing near environment changes. Generic few-shot environment inference or switching-context concerns therefore cannot be claimed as new. The inspected author revision contains papers and README, without runnable code; no Meta-iLaD training was performed. Author-repository and PMLR PDF hashes differ and are recorded separately.

[RP-GSSM, final ICML2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/hromadka26a.html), sections3.1-3.4, distinguishes its empirical-mixture normalization, auxiliary free-energy approximation and Gaussian inference operator. Its identifiability theorem concerns a limiting model under rank/regularity assumptions. The final paper already states linear-Gaussian-prior and approximation-bias limitations and describes a separate post-hoc decoder. Our exact known-likelihood checks qualify the Gaussian component; they do not validate the empirical-RPM objective or discover approximation bias. Earlier C2 training and C46 source audits must be retained in the registry.

One adjacent search lead is [compositional continual world-model learning, September2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.22055v1). It separates action/perception composition and reports that modular PWM's advantage depends on a diagnostic encoder exposed to all tasks. It also discusses task-conditioned routing and uncontrolled capacity. These are author-identified limitations, not our discoveries. Next inspect released PWM/continual-learning implementations and earlier project coverage before designing a time-series mechanism-reuse test. Ordinary replay, alignment, modularity or state-conditioned routing are not independently novel. No new recommendation is accepted from this paper alone.
