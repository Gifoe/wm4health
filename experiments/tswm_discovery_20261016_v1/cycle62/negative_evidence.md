# Negative evidence and limitations

No new research direction is established. At L=H=96 PRO and DYN are exactly the same computation after parameter-key renaming; same-RNG CPU forward and gradients matched exactly in training/evaluation modes. PRO has three completed training seeds; DYN has two auxiliary duplicate fits. This corrects the earlier interpretation note about different modified paths. Four fits use native window copies and four qualified cached copies; three formal attempts crashed. All sealed fits are scored on the native CPU inference path. Do not call a rank change or PRO/DYN duplicate differences a novel mechanism. Existing multistep system identification, state-space simulation and dynamical-system reconstruction are necessary next controls if a residual failure merits further study.

ETTh1 is one previously examined measured system, with overlapping evaluation windows. Training seeds are initialization repeats, not independent systems. This is an exploratory screen. Inference RNG variation comes from native ProbAttention sampling; it is not learned predictive uncertainty. Extended point error alone does not establish incorrect physical dynamics. The source task makes no causal intervention guarantee.

Native model equality is checked on training-only histories before test scoring. Correct native training and a matching independent rollout loop rule out our loop implementation as an explanation of scored differences within stated tolerances; they do not prove convergence, correct stochastic simulation, or absence of future architecture bugs. Native maximum ten epochs and native validation selection are preserved. A stronger trained state-space model is not fitted in this cycle.
