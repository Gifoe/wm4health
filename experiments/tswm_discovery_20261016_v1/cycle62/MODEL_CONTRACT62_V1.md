# What the C62 model call can establish

The native experiment jointly forecasts all seven ETTh1 columns from their past and future calendar marks. It receives no user-specified physical action plan and no future measured load channels. H96 corresponds to four days; H384 to sixteen days at verified hourly spacing.

Therefore this is a conditional point-forecast and block-composition screen. It cannot separate unpredictable future external forcing from errors in a learned autonomous transition law, identify physical states, or validate intervention/counterfactual responses. Correct loop arithmetic does not change this information limit. A future transition-mechanism claim requires a controlled/action-conditioned dataset or an explicitly specified stochastic/exogenous model with appropriate ground truth and controls.

Three inference seeds expose algorithmic random attention only. They must not be scored or advertised as samples of the physical future distribution. A point forecast repeatedly fed back into its own history generally has a different target from a full conditional trajectory law; this is an established distinction, not a newly discovered method.

This limitation is a reason to keep the result as a baseline qualification, not to redefine an original forecasting paper's task after seeing its scores.
