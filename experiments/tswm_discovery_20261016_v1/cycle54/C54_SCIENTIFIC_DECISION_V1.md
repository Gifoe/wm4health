## Hypotheses updated

1. A locally improved prior-gradient/reference diagnostic necessarily predicts improved trained dynamics: falsified in this matched control. Existing True removes one frozen prior-gradient reference effect, yet does not repair the h10 outcome. The flag changes posterior-gradient contracts for multiple parameter blocks, so this is not an isolated causal intervention on the prior gradient alone.
2. High retrospective state recovery alone establishes a useful world simulator: unsupported. The small default task provides a concrete separation from substantially more accurate ordinary conditional predictions. This broad principle is known; the measured instance does not constitute an original research question.
3. A huge mapped Gaussian KL proves an uncertainty-learning gap: rejected as a sufficient inference. The exact-oracle readout counterexample in GAUSSIAN_READOUT_CAVEAT54_V1.md shows that a point decoder can itself manufacture singular or overconfident mapped covariances. Existing scores remain reproducible under their declared diagnostic mapping, but are not clean native-posterior calibration measurements.
4. Existing methods cannot solve these tasks: refuted by the ordinary EM control. This does not show that a generative linear method solves reconstruction-free learning with arbitrary high-dimensional observations.
5. The remaining native deficit proves asymptotic objective inconsistency or refutes the original paper: not established. One fixed budget, one data draw per dimension, an adapted linear launcher and no original full hyperparameter search cannot distinguish optimizer convergence, coupled recognition updates, surrogate approximation and objective bias. The numerical checks do not validate the surrogate as the proper ratio-model posterior.

## Next decisive action

Do not add another architecture or simply extend the same seed sweep. The unresolved mechanistic question is whether the observed learning deficit persists at a controlled population optimum of the implemented objective, after removing finite-reference sampling and optimization failure. Derive a scalar linear-Gaussian case directly from the pinned loss equations and paper assumptions, compare its population/full-reference objective with a proper-likelihood control, and check stationarity/global optima over a prespecified observation-noise range. This is a proposed discriminator, not an executed experiment or a selected final direction. Generic variational bias, running normalizers, stop-gradient EM and Gaussian likelihood learning already have prior art; none may be renamed as the contribution.

If competent optimization of the population/native objective recovers the mechanism, the present objective-bias lead weakens and the next action must address ordinary optimization or change direction. If a systematic population deficit survives, it motivates examining a scalable proper-inference/learning alternative and its nearest prior art. It still needs an actual minimal method plus independent nonlinear or real-system evidence before a framework-level research direction is accepted.

All original12 results, four existing-option controls, six horizons, source versions, failed ordinary-EM attempts, protocol amendments and negative contrasts are preserved. The overall discovery goal remains active and unachieved.
