# Reference-based objectives: novelty and execution boundaries

The current gradient observation is an attribution lead, not a demonstrated new world-model capability.

- [RP-GSSM, ICML2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/hromadka26a.html) already learns recognition and latent dynamics without an explicit decoder. Its final text acknowledges possible bias from its auxiliary approximations. The executed source exposes both total differentiation through the posterior and a fixed-posterior EM option. C54 records which contract is actually used. A failure under the default cannot be attributed to all variants or to an exact proper-RPM likelihood.
- [SogCLR, ICML2022](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/yuan22b.html) already addresses global contrastive objectives at small batch sizes through running normalization estimates. We inspected `sogclr/builder.py` at `Optimization-AI/SogCLR` commit `ef289b52ed72d22d8b3c4e8a76d698c117f02a44`; its gamma default is .9. Our frozen-score adaptation is not an execution of the authors' image-learning pipeline, and it does not test parameter tracking during learning.
- [Gradient-bias study, NeurIPS2022](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/db174d373133dcc6bf83bc98e4b681f8-Abstract-Conference.html) already studies biased minibatch gradients for nondecomposable contrastive losses and a Bayesian augmentation remedy. Generic minibatch gradient bias is therefore not our novelty.
- [Dynamics Contrastive Learning, ICLR2025](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/9ba53f77e348bce55aedffa16d344824-Paper-Conference.pdf) explicitly targets linear, switching and nonlinear system identification, with identifiable latent dynamics under its assumptions. Its [source](https://github.com/dynamical-inference/dcl/tree/2cd46d13ae9e27feeefafe5745f26003929f9c6e) is available. The Table1 script uses 30,000 iterations, 2,048 positives and 20,000 negatives for its LDS configuration; its selected criterion includes the positive in the denominator. Packaged Table1 datasets are explicitly not split by that script. These facts motivate a strong relevant control but do not establish a strict heldout comparison. C54 reviewed source/task contracts only; no DCL training or dataset download is claimed.
- [Contrast encodes inductive bias, June2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07770) already shows that across- versus within-trajectory negative sampling changes whether slow nuisance features or dynamical variables are learned. This is a close boundary against claiming that reference selection affecting dynamics is itself new. Its slow-nuisance shortcut differs from C54's stationary linear-Gaussian training-only gradient fixture; the two mechanisms cannot be equated. The paper/HTML setup was reviewed, not reproduced.
- [No Gaussian Required, August2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17542) was already reviewed and component-audited in C9. Its action-contrastive inverse branch replaces an anti-collapse mechanism under stated action/observability assumptions. That is distinct from the normalizer and inference approximation in a probabilistic recognition-parametrized SSM; it is not a proof that arbitrary stochastic belief learning is solved.

Decision: do not rename a leave-one-out reference, a running normalizer, a source EM switch or ordinary EM as a new world model. Equally, their existence does not prove that every useful combination is solved. A potential contribution must show a useful, reproducible improvement in state/transition or simulation quality beyond these competent controls, at a relevant resource budget and on independent mechanism evidence. The present synthetic gradient diagnostics do not satisfy that requirement.

The next immediate decision is the matched native EM-option learning comparison, declared before any heldout score. It tests a simpler existing-source explanation before any new method is designed. The original C54 task/compute/split contract and all negative controls remain intact.
