# Research branch decision before C83 outcomes

Target: general time-series world models, prospective CCF-A research; no accepted direction. Mode: exploratory, ccf-idea-optimizer. C82 supplied positive local-inference evidence and rejected a simple closure-failure explanation. It did not yet compose a larger simulator.

| Candidate | Lineage / operation | Mechanism and minimum viable question | Main weakness / falsifier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Known-neighborhood pretrained-field composition | C82 / transfer | Can a low-dimensional pretrained field prior support a larger coupled simulator with an actual state intervention and limited noisy context? C83 compares its complete coupled rollouts with ordinary local weak models. | Generic stitching is unoriginal; if strong ordinary identification matches it, do not develop a framework around stitching. **needs-evidence** |
| Observation-transfer system identification | C46 / narrow | Given explicit calibration information and sufficient excitation, can a world model transfer dynamics to an unseen nonlinear sensor layout? | C46 covered known layouts with shared anchors; affine/PCA repairs solved simpler variants. Need a distinct identifiable contract and strong learned-alignment controls. **needs-mechanism, needs-search** |
| Coherent uncertain intervention simulation | C81 / invert | Can uncertainty over inferred fields support decisions under several plans without changing noise semantics? | GP-ODE, WorldKernel, cocycles and TwinRollouts already occupy generic posterior/coherent-coupling claims. A new identifiable decision objective is missing. **needs-mechanism, needs-search** |

Current development route: execute the first controlled screen, with ordinary controls designed to defeat the claim if appropriate. It offers direct new evidence relative to C82, not guaranteed novelty. Different fallback: observation transfer, only after a precise calibration/identifiability contract and theorem/source audit. Do not return to scalar normalization or static known-view anchors under a new name.

Overlap challenge: CHOP already composes frozen operators and simulates PDEs, GICON/UNICON already mix graph and example context. Revision: remove generic compositional-learning novelty; test target-local field reuse with a known explicit intervention on larger systems, and retain all input-information differences.

Evidence challenge: four simple systems, known parents, clean/noisy trajectories cannot validate a foundation world model. Revision: C83 is only a development rejection/continuation screen. The minimum future decisive evidence, conditional on passing, is a preregistered transfer to unseen system mechanisms and sizes with equally informed ordinary graph models. No current candidate is publication-ready.
