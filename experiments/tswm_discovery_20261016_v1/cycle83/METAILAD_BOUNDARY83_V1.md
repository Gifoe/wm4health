# Meta-iLaD: exact scope check for the observation-transfer fallback

Primary: [PMLR paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/ye26p.html), [authoritative PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v306/main/assets/ye26p/ye26p.pdf). PDF inspected in RAM:23pages,14,979,955bytes,SHA256 `6f429a560b4f92c2a33e243fb4e6938e6b62842c2a63b29c4b7f22d32da94f55`. This is a theorem/source reading, not reproduction.

Pages3–5 use a shared invertible observation map and locally stationary environment-conditioned dynamics. The environment prior factorizes as Gaussian coordinates given separate same-environment context trajectories. Theorem1 additionally needs injective trajectory generation, regular sufficient statistics, nonvanishing noise characteristic function almost everywhere, and full-rank variation of2d natural parameters across at least2d+1 contexts. Theorem2 assumes conditional latent-coordinate independence and rank conditions on log-density derivatives across environments; its state equivalence is componentwise invertible up to permutation. Transition identification inherits these transformations, rather than recovering named physical coordinates.

Inference boundary: these conditions do not establish arbitrary unseen sensor-map transfer with no calibration. This leaves a possible different setting, not an established research gap: nonlinear alignment, identifiability and strong ordinary observation-transfer methods still require comparison. The theorem is not invalidated merely by testing outside its assumptions. No criticism of its empirical result follows from this scope distinction. Generic few-shot environment inference is already covered.
