# What the source does and does not establish about the pretraining prior

Source pinned in C80: OpenFIM commit `cee2bb53be1e8e92b73f98dfdbe3605efc9119cc`. Checked existing hash-verified public files directly, without changing them.

`src/fim/data_generation/sde/dynamical_systems_sample.py` around lines202–230 samples initial states and complete drift-parameter realizations until the desired number is reached. `solve_equations`, around lines335–351, rejects an entire realization if any hidden path coordinate is nonfinite; with `reject_threshold` enabled, it also removes a realization unless every sampled path/time/coordinate is below the threshold. The associated complete parameter rows are removed together. `src/fim/data_generation/sde/dynamical_systems.py`, `Polynomials.sample_polynomial_coeffs` around lines873–954, samples coordinate-wise coefficient arrays and degree/monomial masks before this trajectory-level filtering.

This verifies a joint finite-trajectory acceptance event. It is not a proof of asymptotic stability, does not imply that every positive local derivative is excluded, and does not establish the exact retained training distribution without the training-data configuration/corpus. The [FIM-ODE paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.08733v2) already discloses divergence rejection and prior-related limitations. Merely noticing rejection is not novel.

Narrow hypothesis still requiring evidence: conditioning synthetic data on joint trajectory acceptance may couple initially separate mechanism parameters; a target-local estimator deployed under different nuisance-neighbor dynamics may inherit an inappropriate association. The general fact that selection induces dependence is classical. Neither C82 nor C83 isolates this cause. C82 actively contradicts any blanket assertion that open neighborhoods always defeat local inference. Current C83 local errors can reflect ordinary prior mismatch, finite accuracy and support departure instead.

A decisive future attribution would hold the target equation, context support, noise and estimator capacity fixed while changing only joint versus target-mechanism prior conditioning. It must include an exact or well-qualified ordinary posterior/control to distinguish statistical misspecification from neural approximation. Do not start large pretraining or claim a new world-model mechanism until that experiment and a robust-modular-Bayesian/conditional-identification comparison justify it.
