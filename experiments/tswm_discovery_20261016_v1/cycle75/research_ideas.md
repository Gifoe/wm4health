# research ideas

Do not promote sensor calibration itself, the real-data wrapper or the residual NARX baseline as an idea. Sequential reuse remains provisional and needs actual joint-change episodes plus complete observer, nonlinear simulation-error adaptation, replay and capacity controls. The next high-information step is the standard multi-step calibration control, with independent information-boundary and noise checks. A dataset-specific F16 improvement would not satisfy the general-world-model objective.
