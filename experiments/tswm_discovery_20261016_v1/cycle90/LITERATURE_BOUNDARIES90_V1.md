# Prior-art and history reconciliation

This is a focused continuation of C89, not a new broad survey. Current primary-source checks confirm that generic marginalization/query consistency is occupied by [MOSES](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.07246v2), whose queries are time/channel pairs and whose mixture weights depend on observed context rather than the query list. Its [current author README](https://github.com/yalavarthivk/separable_flows) links the ICLR2026 paper. These sources concern irregular forecasting, not an evaluated shared-physics law over distinct action interventions. Changing the query index to an action plan alone would not establish a new principle. MOSES was already recorded in C34/C39; this is confirmation of an existing boundary, not a newly discovered paper.

[PathFM](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.22358v1), submitted2026-10-15, already studies joint trajectory forecasting, downstream path scores and stochastic continuation of chaotic systems. Its v1 reproducibility paragraph states that code/model release is forthcoming; no native checkpoint was executed here. C34/C39 already recorded it. Do not propose generic joint paths, path scoring or stochastic long-run variability as an empty research area.

[OneWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30946) directly studies shared physical mechanisms across action branches. Its exact posterior-ratio construction can preserve marginals ideally. C89's terminal finite-ID Gibbs stress and C90's categorical control are not its native flow-guidance reproduction. [Couple to Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11311) and [EDDY](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06553) already address marginal-preserving dependence design. [OSCC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33543) adds observation-safe branch coupling for policy-gradient estimation. Common noise, Gaussian rotations, ensemble-copula reordering, Bayesian mixtures and IPF cannot be renamed as new methods.

Earlier project evidence also matters: C23–25 already studied Bayesian information updates in six trained stochastic dynamics checkpoints, with mixed actual-quality consequences of incoherent updates. C39 studied fixed-target forecast revisions and existing neural-process/martingale/conditioning-consistency work. Do not restart those generic questions. C10/C11 and later searches covered time irreversibility; [the2026 path-space world-model paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28751) directly discusses operational entropy production. These are not substitutes for the current empirical test.

The remaining working question is a *specific feasible quality–physics–probability–cost tradeoff* in qualified learned time-series simulators. Known primitives do not logically rule out a useful new composition or evaluation contribution, but a different application label is insufficient. Ordinary controls must use comparable available information, and evaluator privilege must be explicit. A known method succeeding in an oracle toy does not prove every learned-model setting solved. Conversely, failure of an undertrained or unsuitable classifier/generator does not establish a research gap.

No new final direction is accepted by this literature check. Full experimental qualification and an independently confirmed practical consequence remain necessary.
